A reproducible comparison

Both implementations use the frozen B2-23 model, the same initial position, and the four published planner seeds. Recorded examples share 160 displacements reconstructed from the published prefix; designed targets start from rest. Target position at millisecond k is received at k ms; output is compared at every closed 1 ms endpoint. The page plays recorded results from these runs.

The upstream showcase publishes rendered integer reports and target guides. Its original target receipt schedule, raw prefixes and Renderer profiles are not fully included. These are fresh, matched smooth-planner reruns of those demo inputs; their paths can differ from the original frozen animations. The comparison covers a finite set of curated demos.

Source versions and measurement details

Position and displacement errors use the full binary64 outputs. Maximum error is the largest absolute X or Y difference. MAE and RMSE use all scalar components. Exact-pair counts require numerical equality on both axes, with no tolerance. All eight timing and decision checks are compared at every sample. Small float differences can accumulate; matching decisions and timing are reported separately.
